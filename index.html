About Me

Curious about data, driven by problems

I’m an aspiring data scientist and data analyst with a passion for uncovering the story hidden inside data. With a B.Sc. in Chemistry and an ongoing diploma in Data Science, I bring a scientific, evidence-first mindset to every dataset I work with.

I enjoy the full analytical journey: cleaning and exploring data, asking sharper questions, building machine learning models, and communicating findings through clear visualizations and dashboards.

My earlier supervisory experience in a customer-facing role strengthened my communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills, which I now apply to turning complex data into decisions people can act on.
